## What this site does not collect

- The site has **no contact form**; you do not submit any information through it.
- The site **sets no cookies** and stores no tracking data in your browser.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking tools** (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, etc.) are used.
- Fonts and images are served from the site itself; no requests are sent to third-party servers.

## Server logs

The site is published through a hosting service. As with any website, when you open a page the hosting provider may briefly process technical access data such as your IP address, date and time, and browser type, for security and to keep the service running. This data is not used to identify you.

## When you contact me

When you write to me via WhatsApp, email or Instagram, I receive your name, your phone number or email address, and whatever you choose to share – messages, measurements, plans and photos.

- **Purpose:** to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if we agree to work together, deliver the design service.
- **Legal basis:** necessity for entering into or performing a contract, and the legitimate interest in answering your enquiry (KVKK Art. 5/2).
- **Sharing:** your data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing. It may only be disclosed to authorities where the law requires it.
- **Retention:** your data is kept for as long as your enquiry or project requires and for statutory retention periods, and is deleted afterwards.

## Third-party services

Clicking the WhatsApp or Instagram links on this site takes you to those platforms. From that point on, your data is processed under the privacy policies of the respective platforms (Meta Platforms), over which I have no control.

## Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to:

- learn whether your personal data is being processed and request information about it,
- learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used accordingly,
- know the third parties to whom the data is transferred,
- request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data,
- request deletion or destruction of your data,
- claim compensation if you suffer damage as a result of unlawful processing.

To exercise these rights, write to [a.yilmazkan35@gmail.com](mailto:a.yilmazkan35@gmail.com). Requests are answered within 30 days at the latest.
